Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026. Applies to vantflow.tech and to work delivered by VANTFLOW.

Short version. This website has no contact form, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts, and it sets no cookies. It cannot identify you. Three parties do see technical request data when you load a page: GitHub, which hosts the files, and Google, which serves the two web fonts. If you then message us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep that conversation so we can answer it.

1. Who is responsible

VANTFLOW is a one-person automation practice run by Mohammed Anes, based in Algeria and working with clients remotely. For anything in this policy, write to anesch829@gmail.com.

2. What this website collects by itself

Nothing. There is no form, no login, no newsletter, no comment box, no analytics tool, no advertising pixel and no chat widget. The site is a set of static files with one small script that fades sections in as you scroll; it stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No cookies are set by vantflow.tech, and nothing is written to your browser storage.

3. What our hosting provider records

The site is published with GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives the technical details of each request: your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser and operating system version, and the referring page. It keeps them in server logs to serve the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not have access to those logs and cannot read them. GitHub acts as our hosting processor; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement and the GitHub Terms of Service.

4. Fonts served by Google

The page loads three typefaces (IBM Plex Sans, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono) from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. That request reveals your IP address and browser details to Google, in the United States, before the page finishes rendering. Google states it does not use Google Fonts requests to build advertising profiles; see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ. If you would rather this did not happen, block those two domains. The site stays fully readable with your system fonts. We are aware that self-hosting the fonts would remove this transfer entirely, and it is on our list.

5. When you contact us

Both contact buttons hand you over to a service you already use:

  • WhatsApp opens a chat with the number +213 791 192 350. The conversation is held on WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta and governed by Meta's own privacy terms. We see your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and whatever you write.
  • Email opens your own mail application addressed to anesch829@gmail.com, a Gmail mailbox hosted by Google. We see your email address, your name if your mail client sends one, and the message.

We use those details only to reply and to run the project you are asking about. We keep an enquiry for up to 24 months so we can pick a conversation back up, then delete it. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not pass your details to anyone else.

6. Project data during a paid engagement

This is the part that matters most, so it is stated plainly. Automations are built inside your accounts: your n8n instance, your CRM, your spreadsheets, your messaging channels. We do not run a platform that your data flows through, and we do not copy your records into our own systems.

  • To build and test, you grant us temporary access to the specific tools involved. That access is yours to revoke, and we ask you to revoke it at handover.
  • Where a workflow uses a language model, the text that workflow sends is processed by that provider's API under your account and their terms. We name every such provider before we build, and we agree with you exactly which fields are allowed to leave your systems. Nothing is used to train a model by us.
  • Test data: we work from a small sample you choose. Real customer records are used only when a realistic test needs them, and the sample is deleted once the workflow is accepted.
  • Credentials and API keys are stored in your own tool's credential store, never in our notes, never in code, and never in a message.
  • If a project makes us a processor of personal data on your behalf, we will sign a written data processing agreement with you.

7. What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not run advertising or behavioural tracking. We do not build profiles of visitors. We do not publish a client's data, screenshots of their records, or their name as a reference without written permission.

8. Legal basis

Where the GDPR or a comparable law applies to you, we rely on legitimate interest for serving and protecting the website, and on performance of a contract (or the steps leading up to one) for handling your enquiry and running your project. There is no processing here that requires consent, because there is nothing to consent to: no cookies, no tracking, no marketing list.

9. Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email anesch829@gmail.com with enough detail to find the record. The phone number or email address you used is usually enough. We answer within 30 days. Because we hold so little, the honest answer to most requests is a short one. Requests about server logs must go to GitHub, and requests about a WhatsApp conversation to Meta, since we cannot delete data on their systems.

10. International transfers

VANTFLOW operates from Algeria. GitHub, Google and Meta are United States companies with global infrastructure, so loading this site or messaging us involves a transfer outside your country. Those transfers happen under each provider's own safeguards, which are described in the policies linked above.

11. Children

This is a business-to-business service and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 16.

12. Security, honestly stated

The website is static and served over HTTPS, which removes most of the usual attack surface: there is no database, no login and no user input. Enquiries live in Gmail and WhatsApp accounts protected by two-factor authentication. No system is perfect, and we will not pretend otherwise; if a breach ever affects your data we will tell you what happened and what we did about it.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous version stays visible in the public commit history of the site's repository. We do not silently rewrite it.
